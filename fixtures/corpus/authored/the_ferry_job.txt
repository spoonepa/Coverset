THE FERRY JOB
An authored breakdown fixture. Original text, written for coverset.

Why this file exists (BRK-011): the corpus tier proves the parser survives real
formatting but cannot say the records are correct, because a downloaded script has no
answer key. This one does -- the scene list was written first and the pages written to
match -- so the offline suite can assert correctness against it. It carries, on purpose:
a mix of numbered and unnumbered headings (BRK-014), a CONTINUOUS heading that states no
time of day, a heading naming two places (BRK-013), and stunt, VFX and minor flags
(BRK-002).


1   INT. MAYA'S APARTMENT - NIGHT

Rain on the window. MAYA counts a thin stack of bills at the table. Three
knocks. She doesn't look up.

MAYA
It's open.

DEV steps in, soaked through.

DEV
We move at first light. You in?

MAYA
I was in before you knocked.


2   EXT. BROOKLYN BRIDGE PARK - DAY

Joggers. Gulls. MAYA and DEV walk the rail above the water.

Without breaking stride DEV vaults the railing, drops eight feet to the
maintenance walk below, and lands clean. A stunt, timed to the second.

DEV
Nobody follows us across.

MAYA
Show-off.


INT. WAREHOUSE - CONTINUOUS

Crates to the ceiling. RUTH waits on an upturned pallet, checking her watch.

RUTH
You're late. The tide doesn't wait and neither do I.


EXT. FERRY TERMINAL / RIVER DOCK - DUSK

The last ferry noses into the slip. Behind it, impossibly, a second identical
ferry glides through -- a visual-effects plate to be composited in post.

MAYA
That's our ride. Both of them.


3   INT. MAYA'S APARTMENT - DAY

Morning light now. A KID, no older than nine, hides under the same table,
knees to chest.

MAYA
Stay down, and stay quiet. Whatever you hear.

The KID nods, wide-eyed.

FADE OUT.
